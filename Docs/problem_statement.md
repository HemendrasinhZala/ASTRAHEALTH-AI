# ASTRAHEALTH AI

## Project Title
Autonomous Astronaut Health Monitoring and Decision Support System

## Problem Statement
Astronauts participating in long-duration space missions are exposed to multiple health risks, including space radiation, microgravity-related physiological changes, sleep disturbances, psychological stress and environmental challenges.

Traditional health monitoring approaches may not provide sufficient personalized, continuous and intelligent analysis of these multiple health indicators.

ASTRAHEALTH AI aims to develop an intelligent health-monitoring platform that integrates physiological, behavioural and environmental data to identify potential abnormalities, monitor health trends and provide explainable decision-support information.

## Main Objectives

1. Collect and integrate astronaut health-related data.
2. Establish individual health baselines.
3. Detect abnormal health patterns using Machine Learning.
4. Monitor multiple dimensions of astronaut health.
5. Integrate NASA research for scientific context.
6. Generate explainable health alerts and recommendations.
7. Support offline-capable monitoring for space missions.

## Proposed Innovation

A multi-dimensional astronaut health intelligence system combining personalized ML-based anomaly detection, NASA research-grounded Generative AI and an autonomous monitoring workflow.

## Target Users
- Astronauts
- Mission Control teams
- Space medicine researchers

## Expected Outcome
An interactive AI-powered health monitoring platform that demonstrates astronaut health tracking, anomaly detection, explainable alerts and research-supported decision assistance.

## Important Limitation
The prototype is a research and decision-support demonstration, not a clinically validated medical diagnostic system.
